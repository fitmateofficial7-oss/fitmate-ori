# FitMate AI v14.13

## Locked quota actions

- Workout-plan generation now shows a locked Premium state after the Free lifetime quota is exhausted.
- The locked generate button remains clickable only to open the Premium page; it cannot call the generation API.
- Free AI consultation input, suggestions, and send action are locked after the lifetime consultation is used.
- Free meal-photo upload, notes, change-photo, and analysis actions are locked after the lifetime scan is used.
- Clicking any locked Coach action opens Premium with the appropriate feature source parameter.
- Premium daily-limit behavior remains unchanged: it shows the daily reset state and does not incorrectly sell another Premium subscription.
